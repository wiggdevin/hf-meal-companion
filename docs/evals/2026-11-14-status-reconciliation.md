## Intake-error result (WP3 / H2)

The [new backtest](2026-11-15-exp-3.1.0-backtest.md) adds five deterministic log-error scenarios. Each keeps true intake and expenditure at 2,130 kcal/day and weight flat. After the seed fades, constant −15% logging produces a −320 kcal estimate error; omitting 120 kcal of cooking fat produces a −120 kcal error. Falsely marking every fifth day complete while omitting 600 kcal yields a −110 to −130 kcal error. All those mature estimates remain proposal-eligible. A −15% logging bias starting on day 45 causes a false expenditure step; only day 59 temporarily blocks proposal eligibility. Seeded random ±120 kcal error stays within its tested ±125 kcal envelope including display rounding; this seed's measured mature error is −10 to +30 kcal.

These results support H2 under the fixture assumptions. A persistent logging bias transfers into learned TDEE. The engine cannot infer omitted energy from a flat weight series and internally consistent biased logs. These checks exercise numerical behavior, not clinical accuracy, target approval or outcomes. They do not justify an engine coefficient change.
